How to play

The 8 × 5 board holds 36 Dot tiles, three Dragons and one Key. The locked chest covers four center tiles. Each tile flips face up when it enters the tray.

Pick the Key to open the chest and reveal four tiles you can choose from. The Key skips the tray.

Fill the five-slot tray to check for a set. A set clears and frees space for more picks. A straight pays 0.5 times the bet before the combo multiplier. The round ends when no set can be formed or you cash out.

The combo multiplier starts at ×1 each round. Each paying set doubles the multiplier for the next set, up to ×8.

Auto-play picks tiles at random and starts new rounds until you turn it off or run out of balance.
